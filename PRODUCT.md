## Platform

## Stack

## Users

- **[Inferred from the supplied brief]** Primary users are AI/full-stack learners and builders who want to understand prompt iteration and apply it to practical work.

## Product Purpose

- **[Inferred from the supplied brief]** PromptCraft is a learning showcase that makes the difference between zero-shot output and refined, production-ready work easy to inspect.
- Success means a visitor can compare the initial and refined artifact, understand the refinement decision, and reuse the prompt/system pattern.

## Positioning

- **[Inferred from the supplied brief]** The portal teaches prompt engineering through authentic before/after case studies from an instructional slide curriculum, not generic prompt tips or invented portfolio claims.

## Operating Context

- Visitors browse case-study modules by category.
- Visitors open a comparison dialog, switch between initial and refined outputs, read the five-step iteration flow, inspect syntax, and copy the visible prompt.
- The portal is evaluated as a responsive browser experience on desktop and mobile web.
